# FAQ

**Do I need a degree?**
No. Most vulnerability-research job postings sampled in [research/benchmarks/job-market.md](research/benchmarks/job-market.md) say "or equivalent experience" or do not mention a degree. What they ask for is OS internals, reverse engineering, code audit, clear writing and evidence of a real find.

**How long does it take?**
Nobody publishes reliable hours from zero to a first 0-day, so OSVR does not guess. The [curricula research](research/benchmarks/curricula-and-trainings.md) shows a university semester reaches stack, ROP and heap basics.

**Can I skip sections?**
Skip Programming if you can already write a 100-line program unaided. Otherwise go in order: each section's milestone is the check that you are ready for the next.

**Why so much pwn.college and OpenSecurityTraining2?**
They are free, hands-on, automatically checked, and cover the core path in a consistent order. Where something else is better for a topic, it is listed instead.

**Why are some resources paid?**
Where a paid book or course is clearly the best option for a step, it is listed, marked with its price, and paired with a free alternative where one exists. Every Core row in sections 1 to 5 can be done for free. The CS:APP labs are free to download, but the textbook that goes with them is paid.

**Why do some resources use old mitigations?**
Some classics (Exploit Education Phoenix, RPISEC MBE) deliberately turn mitigations off or predate current defaults. They are marked in the Notes column. Use them to learn mechanisms, then practise against current defaults.

**Can I practise on real websites or devices?**
Only on systems you own, CTF/wargame infrastructure, archived vulnerable versions you run locally, or inside a published bug-bounty scope. Read your local law and the programme's safe-harbour terms.

**Is there a community?**
Not yet. Open an issue.
